ABOUT

Three roles, one thread.

I'm Aurajeet Mahapatra. Two years across five state-level political campaigns — electoral analytics across 65,000+ polling booths, growth loops that crossed 1.2M+ signups, and advisory to senior leadership; all five campaigns won. Then a year at HEBE, rebuilding a B2B essential-oil business with a 5,000-farmer supplier network into a digitally instrumented supply chain — ₹1.6 Cr to ₹2.1 Cr in twelve months. The thread across all three: shipping products under hard constraints with imperfect data.

BIT Mesra (2019–2023)

I studied mechanical engineering at the Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, and graduated with a CGPA of 8.84. The degree taught me first-principles thinking and how to decompose unfamiliar systems into pieces small enough to reason about — habits I still rely on every week.

Nation With NaMo (Jul 2023 – Jul 2025)

I joined Nation With NaMo as an Associate Consultant and worked across five state-level electoral campaigns over two years — Madhya Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Odisha, and West Bengal. All five campaigns won. The role ran in three modes — analyst, consultant, and product — across three workstreams: the political war room (an electoral analytics dashboard covering 65,000+ polling booths and a geospatial clustering model across 1.5M+ voter profiles, synthesized into recommendations to senior political leadership on candidate selection, narrative direction, and manifesto priorities), the campaign war room (growth and content strategy across three campaigns, a 12-person cross-functional team, and 1.2M+ signups), and governance advisory (a Pink Booth campaign that lifted female voter turnout 38% in rural Ratlam, and a Forest Rights Act digitization initiative that secured land titles for 2,800+ tribal families within nine months).

HEBE (Jul 2025 – present)

I joined HEBE — a B2B essential-oil manufacturing and trading business with a 5,000+ farmer supplier network, 100+ workers, and almost no digital infrastructure — as Product and Operations Lead. The domain wasn't unfamiliar: I'd been volunteering with Sabuja Biplav, an essential-oil farmer-livelihood project in Odisha, since 2020, and had helped secure ₹30+ lakhs in government and NABARD funding for adjacent programs and corporate CSR partnerships with doTERRA and Oriplast. My job at HEBE was to take revenue from ₹1.6 Cr to something defensible and operate the business as if it were a software product: instrumented, prioritized, and shipped on cycles. In twelve months I built a B2B marketplace with farmer-level traceability, integrated payments and operational workflows, and rebuilt pricing, supply-demand alignment, and the go-to-market strategy. We ended the year at ₹2.1 Cr — 31% YoY growth.

Why product, why now

Product management is the synthesis of what every previous role has rehearsed in pieces: deciding what to build, deciding what to measure, owning the outcome — and forcing fragmented evidence into a decision someone could act on. After five years across engineering, growth and advisory work, and B2B operations, the move to product is a naming, not a pivot. The case studies on this site are the work the move is built on.

What I'm looking for

Associate Product Manager or Product Manager roles where I can learn from a strong product team while owning a meaningful surface area end-to-end. 0-to-1 or growth problems preferred.

Tools & methods

SQL · Power BI · Excel · Claude Code · AI Prototyping · Product Instrumentation. Plus stakeholder management, business statistics, and the operating discipline that ships under tight cycles. AI tools are productivity multipliers; they don't replace the work of getting close to users and the data.

Open to product roles. Always happy to have a conversation first.
